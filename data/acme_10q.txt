ITEM 2. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS OF FINANCIAL CONDITION AND RESULTS OF OPERATIONS

Overview

Acme Corp reported strong growth in the third quarter. Revenue rose to $4.2B, driven by cloud
services. Operating margin improved to 21% as data-center costs fell. Guidance for Q4 assumes
continued demand in enterprise accounts and stable component pricing.

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights

- Total revenue was $4,212 million, up 14.3% year over year.
- Cloud Services revenue was $2,398 million, up 26.1% year over year.
- Operating income was $885 million, and operating margin was 21.0%, compared with
  16.1% in the third quarter of 2025.
- Net income was $731 million, or $1.43 per diluted share.
- Operating cash flow for the first nine months was $4,191 million; capital expenditures were $3,214 million.

Results of Operations

Revenue. Total revenue increased $528 million, or 14.3%, compared with
the third quarter of 2025. The increase was driven primarily by Cloud Services, where consumption from
existing enterprise customers grew and new AI inference workloads ramped. Enterprise Software revenue
grew 6.1% on higher subscription renewals. Devices and Other revenue declined
6.2% following the exit of two low-margin hardware lines in the first quarter.

Cost of revenue and gross margin. Cost of revenue was $1,727 million, and gross margin was
59.0%, compared with 56.0% in the third quarter of 2025. Data-center costs per unit of
compute fell as newer facilities with lower power costs came online and server utilization improved.

Operating expenses. Research and development expense was $842 million, reflecting continued
investment in cloud infrastructure and AI products. Sales and marketing expense was $533 million.
General and administrative expense was $225 million, including legal costs described in Part II, Item 1.

Operating margin. Operating margin was 21.0% in the third quarter of 2026, up from
16.1% a year earlier. The improvement came mainly from
lower data-center costs and operating leverage on higher Cloud Services revenue.

Other income and taxes. Other income, net, was $18 million. The effective tax rate was
approximately 19%.

Liquidity and Capital Resources
